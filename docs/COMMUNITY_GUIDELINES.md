# Community Guidelines

**AthleteBeats**
**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026

---

## Our Mission

AthleteBeats is a platform where athletes, fans, coaches, and media come together through the shared language of sports and music. To keep this space positive, authentic, and safe for everyone, we ask all members to follow these Community Guidelines.

Violations may result in content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent ban — including blocking your phone number and email from ever creating a new account.

---

## ✅ Be Yourself

- Use your real identity or a consistent persona
- Do not impersonate athletes, celebrities, coaches, or public figures
- Only verified accounts (✓) are confirmed as the real person
- Do not claim to be verified if you are not

---

## 🤝 Respect Others

- Treat every member with basic human respect regardless of their role, team, sport, background, or opinion
- Disagreements are fine — personal attacks are not
- No harassment, bullying, or coordinated targeting of another user
- Do not send unsolicited messages of a sexual nature
- Do not publicly post another person's private information (addresses, phone numbers, emails)

---

## 🚫 Zero Tolerance

The following will result in **immediate permanent ban** with no warning:

- Sexual content involving minors (CSAM) — will also be reported to authorities
- Threats of real-world violence against any person
- Sharing another user's private location or contact information with intent to harm
- Coordinated harassment campaigns
- Creating accounts to circumvent an existing ban

---

## 🎵 Music & Content

- Only share music through the official Spotify or SoundCloud integrations
- Do not upload or distribute copyrighted audio files directly
- Give credit when sharing another artist's work
- Do not post content that exists only to promote a product or service without transparency (spam)

---

## 🏆 Sports Integrity

- Do not spread false information about game results, player injuries, or team news
- Do not use AthleteBeats to facilitate illegal sports betting
- Celebrate competition — do not celebrate player injuries or wish harm on athletes

---

## 🗣️ Free Expression — With Limits

We believe in open discussion about sports, music, culture, and life. You can:
- Debate sports opinions passionately
- Criticize teams, coaches, and public figures' professional conduct
- Share controversial takes on games, music, and culture

You cannot:
- Post hate speech targeting a group by race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin
- Spread medical or health misinformation
- Post graphic violence or gore

---

## 🤖 No Bots or Automation

- Do not use bots, scripts, or automation tools to interact with the platform
- Do not artificially inflate likes, followers, or engagement
- Accounts found running automated activity will be banned

---

## 📣 Reporting

If you see content that violates these guidelines:
- Use the report button on any post, profile, or comment
- Our moderation team (and AI moderation system) will review reports within 24–48 hours
- Do not try to take matters into your own hands — report and let us handle it

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

| Violation Level | Action |
|-----------------|--------|
| Minor / First offense | Warning + content removal |
| Repeated minor violations | Temporary suspension (1–30 days) |
| Serious violation | Immediate suspension, review |
| Severe / Zero tolerance | Permanent ban (email + phone blocked) |

We reserve the right to make enforcement decisions at our discretion. Appeals can be sent to bradnifisent@gmail.com.
