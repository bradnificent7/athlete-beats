## 1. Introduction

AthleteBeats ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using AthleteBeats, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
When you create an account or use the Platform, we collect:
- **Email address** — used for authentication and account recovery
- **Phone number** — used for SMS verification (optional)
- **Username and display name** — shown publicly on your profile
- **User role** — Fan, Athlete, Coach, or Media
- **Bio and profile information** — displayed publicly on your profile
- **Content you post** — songs, quotes, reactions, comments, stories

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
When you use the Platform, we automatically collect:
- **Device information** — browser type, operating system
- **IP address** — used for security and abuse prevention
- **Usage data** — pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Login activity** — timestamps, devices used for login

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
When you sign in with Google or connect music services, we may receive:
- **Google account info** — name, email, profile photo (if using Google sign-in)
- **Spotify/SoundCloud data** — music preferences and listening activity (when connected)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate your identity and prevent unauthorized access
- Display your profile and content to other users
- Show you relevant sports stats, music, and news
- Enable social features (following, reactions, stories)
- Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud and abuse
- Improve the Platform through analytics
- Send you important account notifications (not marketing spam)

---

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with:
- **Firebase (Google)** — our authentication and database provider
- **Spotify / SoundCloud** — when you connect your music account
- **Sports data providers** — BallDontLie, API-Sports (receive no personal data)
- **Law enforcement** — if required by law or to protect user safety
- **Service providers** — who assist in operating the Platform under confidentiality agreements

### Public Information
The following is visible to all users by default:
- Your username and display name
- Your profile photo and bio
- Your role badge (Fan, Athlete, Coach, Media)
- Your posts, reactions, and public activity
- Your follower and following counts

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:
- Your profile and public posts will be removed within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for up to 90 days for security and fraud prevention purposes
- Data required by law may be retained longer

**Banned accounts:** We retain email addresses and phone numbers of permanently banned users to enforce our ban policy. This data is never used for any other purpose.

You have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction** — update inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your account and data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a portable format
- **Opt out** — of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at bradnifisent@gmail.com.

## 7. Children's Privacy

AthleteBeats is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected data from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us at bradnifisent@gmail.com.

## 8. Security

We take security seriously. We use:
- Firebase Authentication (industry-standard encryption)
- Environment variables to protect API keys
- Firestore Security Rules to restrict data access
- HTTPS encryption on all connections

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
